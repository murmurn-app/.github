Murmurn is a web app where you follow crypto traders whose profit is verified on-chain, then trade or copy their trades in USDC on Solana. Sign in with Google and you have a wallet. Network fees are paid in USDC inside the same transaction, so you never need to hold SOL.

### What it does

- **Trust Score.** Every trader is rated from 0 to 100 on realised profit, consistency and trade count. Only closed trades count.
- **Proof of PnL.** Each counted trade is checked against the blockchain and links to its transaction.
- **Copy trading.** Copy a trader and Murmurn places the trade for you, sized to your own limits.
- **Rug Shield.** Watches the tokens you hold and sells to USDC if the mint authority changes or liquidity is pulled, within limits you set.
- **Shill Check.** Every post shows whether its author still holds the token, with the return since it went up.
- **Flock Mode.** One alert when several traders you follow buy the same token inside a window you choose.

### Fees

0.52% per trade plus a flat 0.02 USDC, shown in full before you confirm. Minimum trade is 20 USDC.
